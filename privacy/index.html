Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Emberforge Works ("Emberforge Works," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit emberforgeworks.com (the "Site") or contact us through it. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide to us. When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you choose to share — your name, email address, optional phone number, and the details you provide about the problem you're trying to solve, what you've tried, and your timeline. We only collect this information when you choose to send it to us.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically through analytics tools, including pages viewed, time spent on the Site, the website or source that referred you, and general details about your device and browser (such as device type, operating system, and approximate location derived from your IP address). This information is collected in aggregate to help us understand how the Site is used.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate with you about your project or potential engagement;
  • Provide, operate, and improve the Site and our services;
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can make it better; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to serve advertising.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand Site usage. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect and analyze information about how visitors interact with the Site. This information is processed in a way that does not directly identify you.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Disabling cookies may affect how parts of the Site function.

4. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. We use trusted third parties to operate our Site and business, including website hosting, email delivery, and analytics. These providers process information only on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services. They include providers such as our hosting platform, our email delivery provider, and Google Analytics.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect our rights, or protect the safety of others.
  • Business transfers. If Emberforge Works is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Data retention

We retain the information you submit through the contact form for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for our legitimate business purposes, after which it may be deleted. Analytics data is retained in accordance with our analytics provider's standard retention settings.

6. Your choices and rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have provided to us by contacting us at the email address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws regarding your personal information. We will respond to such requests as required by law.

You can also limit collection by adjusting your browser's cookie settings or using the analytics opt-out described above.

7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Third-party links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
