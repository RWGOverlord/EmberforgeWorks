Before

Megan built her practice on six tools that didn't share data: Airtable, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Canva, SignWell, and printed intake packets. Every client's details lived in several places at once, and asking a family for a late payment meant writing that awkward message herself.

Questions

Before you book a call.

What happens on the free call?

About 30 minutes. You walk through what's slowing the business down and what you've tried so far, and we work out whether custom software is the right answer and roughly where it would land on the pricing above.

If it's a fit, the next step is a $1,500 Blueprint, credited in full toward your build. If it isn't, there's no obligation.

Who owns the software when it's done?

You do. Once the build is paid in full, the system, its source code, and your data belong to you, running on your own domain.

Emberforge brings a set of reusable tools and components to every project, and your system includes a permanent license to use them. If you need exclusivity in your market, that's scoped and priced in the Blueprint.

How long does a build take?

The Blueprint usually takes 1–2 weeks. Builds typically run 4–6 weeks for Launch, 6–10 weeks for Workflow, and 10–16 weeks for an Operations Platform. Custom Platform builds are scoped individually.

Could an off-the-shelf tool or no-code app do this instead?

Sometimes, and when one fits your needs it will usually beat a custom build on cost, support, and reliability. Custom software earns its keep when your process is stitched together from tools that don't talk to each other, when you're bending the business to fit someone else's template, or when the real work lives in spreadsheets on the side.

The free call is a good place to find out which situation you're in. There's a longer breakdown in When Custom Software Makes Sense for a Small Business.

Why not hire a freelancer, an offshore team, or my own developer?

You can, and the Blueprint is written so you could hand it to any of them. Working with Emberforge means a fixed price before the build starts, working software to review at every stage, and one person who both maps your process and builds the system, so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

You work directly with the founder, based in Chattanooga, TN, on Eastern time.

If AI can write code, why not build it myself?

AI tools make writing code faster, and Emberforge uses them. They don't decide what the system should do, how your data should be structured, or who's allowed to see what, and they don't keep it secure and running once your clients depend on it. That judgment is what the Blueprint and the build are for.

What happens after launch?

Every package includes 90 days of post-launch support. After that, the optional Care Plan covers managed hosting and priority bug fixes from $450/month, month to month. New features are scoped and quoted separately. Your ownership of the system doesn't depend on it.
